ASHWINI D — DIGITAL MARKETING PORTFOLIO

PREVIEW
1. Extract this ZIP folder.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).
3. Keep the assets folder next to index.html so the portrait, certificates, reports and CV load correctly.

INCLUDED
- Responsive portfolio with a refined graphite, lime and mint theme
- Animated hero, floating information cards, scroll reveal and progress indicator
- About, performance funnel, skills, education, certificates and campaign case studies
- Interactive Campaign Lab with illustrative click/lead estimates
- Certificate lightbox, campaign detail modals and email-draft contact form
- CV download and supplied portfolio evidence

IMPORTANT DATA NOTES
- Campaign figures are transcribed from the reports supplied for this portfolio and have not been independently verified.
- The Campaign Lab is illustrative only. Estimates depend on entered assumptions and are not actual campaign results or guarantees.
- Reported lead-objective results should not be treated as proof of qualified leads or completed sales.
- The contact form opens an email draft; it does not submit data to a server.

HOSTING
This is a static website. To publish it, upload the folder contents to a static host such as Vercel, Netlify or your own web hosting. It has not been deployed publicly by this package.
